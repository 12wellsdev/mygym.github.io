Questions

Frequently Asked

01Is the gym really open 24/7?

Yes — MyGym never closes. Members access the facility via the GymDesk app, every hour of every day, including all holidays.

02What's in the VIP Room?

Sauna, cold plunge, UV tanning booth, and massage chair — exclusive to VIP members.

03How do I access the gym?

Entry is handled through the GymDesk app. After signing up, you'll receive instructions to download the app — it's your key to the facility 24/7. Payments are also managed through GymDesk, keeping everything in one place.

04What makes MyGym different?

We built MyGym around you — your goals, your schedule, your My. 24/7 access, no restrictions, VIP recovery that most gyms can't match.

05Is there staff overnight?

The facility is monitored 24/7 via security cameras. Contact us for staffed hour schedule. App access works at all times regardless.

06What is #WhatsYourMy?

The question at the heart of everything we do. What drives you to show up? Your My is yours — we just give you the space to chase it.
